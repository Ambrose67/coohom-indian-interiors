# Rooms, units, doors and ceilings

All sizes in mm. **(rule of thumb)** = practice convention; **(check catalogue)** = brand/model dependent.

## Living / hall
- Main walkway 900–1200; secondary 600–750. Sofa to coffee table 400–450.
- Sofa: 3-seater 1950–2200 wide, 850–950 deep, seat 420–450 high. Coffee table 400–450 high.
- Allow an open floor patch of about 1800 × 1800 or more when the family sits on the floor or hosts functions.
- Tamil Nadu favourites: wooden oonjal (swing) — check the slab hook point with the engineer; Kerala: wooden ceiling panels and brass lamps.

## TV unit
- Floating console 350–450 deep, mounted 300–450 above the floor; wall panel behind (laminate, veneer, fluted, stone).
- TV centre about 1000–1100 above the floor for seated viewing; viewing distance about 1.5–2.5 × screen diagonal.
- Concealed conduit from the console to the TV for HDMI, power, set-top box and router; sockets about 450 above floor or behind the TV.

## Crockery unit
- Base 400–450 deep with drawers; upper glass-shuttered unit 300–350 deep; overall height about 2100.
- Glass shelves need edge support; profile-light inside is common.

## Shoe rack
- 300–350 deep, 900–1050 high (can include a seat); near or outside the main door.
- Ventilated shutters (louvres/perforated) to avoid odour; tilted shoe shelves save depth (check catalogue for pull-out shoe racks).

## Pooja
- Wall unit 600–1200 wide, 300–450 deep; floor mandir 900–1200 wide.
- Deity platform about 900–1100 high for standing worship, 600–900 for seated (floor) worship.
- Pooja room about 4' × 4' to 5' × 6'; jaali or bell doors so deities stay visible; marble/Corian/wood platform; drawer for pooja items; vent for lamp smoke; warm light.
- Vastu option: north-east. Ask the family about deity layout and door style; do not assume.

## Bedroom
- Beds: King 1830 × 1980, Queen 1525 × 1980, Single 915 × 1905; height 450–500 with storage (hydraulic or box).
- 600 minimum beside a bed; 750–900 at the foot if it is a walkway.
- Dressing unit 600–900 wide; mirror bottom about 1000–1100, top ≥ 1800–1900.
- Study table 750 high, 500–600 deep; 1000–1200 wide.
- Bedside switches/sockets 600–750 above floor.

## Bathroom
- Vanity: rim 800–850; WPC/PVC or BWP ply carcass; 750 clear in front.
- WC centre 400–450 from side wall, 600 clear in front. Shower zone 900 × 900 minimum, 1200 × 900 better.
- Geyser 1800–2000 high; exhaust high on the external wall.

## Doors

| Door | Size (W × H) | Common shutter / frame |
|---|---|---|
| Main | 1000–1200 × 2100 | Solid teak or teak-faced shutter; teak or hardwood frame; mortise lock, tower bolts, viewer |
| Bedroom | 900 × 2100 | Flush door with laminate or veneer; hardwood or engineered frame |
| Kitchen | 900 × 2100 | Flush door or open arch |
| Toilet | 750 × 2100 | FRP, WPC or PVC shutter (water-resistant); WPC frame; granite threshold |
| Pooja | per design | Carved wood, bell or jaali doors |

Door lintel level 2100. Check every swing in plan against furniture.

## False ceilings
- Default: 12.5 mm gypsum board on a GI frame. POP in some places; wooden panelling popular in Kerala; PVC/WPC in bathrooms and sit-outs.
- Typical drop 225–300 for coves and lights. Aim to keep at least about 2600 clear (rule of thumb):
  - **9 ft (2743)**: a full drop leaves under 2600 — use a peripheral (border) ceiling or cove only, keep the centre at slab.
  - **10 ft (3048)** and **11 ft (3353)**: full false ceilings with coves are fine.
- Coordinate with wardrobes and lofts: either run the wardrobe to the slab and stop the false ceiling at its face, or stop the wardrobe under the false ceiling — decide before the ceiling is framed.
- Plan AC, fan hook points and light positions before the ceiling is closed.

## Lighting
- Lux targets: living 150–300, bedroom 100–150, kitchen counter 300–500, study 300–500, bath 150–200, corridor 100.
- Colour temperature: 2700–3000 K living/bedrooms; about 4000 K kitchen, study, bath.
- Layers: ambient (cove, panels), task (under-cabinet, reading), accent (spots, profiles).
- Switchboards 1200–1350; split AC point 2100–2300; geyser point about 2000.
