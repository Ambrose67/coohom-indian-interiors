# Materials, finishes, hardware and colour

## Boards

| Board | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| MR plywood (moisture resistant, IS 303) | Wardrobes, TV units, beds, dry-area furniture | Anything near water |
| BWR / BWP plywood (BWP = boiling-water proof, IS 710 marine) | Kitchens (at least sink and hob bases), bathroom vanities, utility, units on damp external walls, RCC loft linings. In Kerala's humidity, BWP wherever in doubt | — |
| HDHMR (high-density, high-moisture-resistant board) | Shutters, CNC-routed fronts, budget kitchens | Load-bearing long spans without support |
| MDF | Dry decorative work, painted panels | Kitchens, bathrooms, damp walls (swells) |
| WPC / PVC board | Bathroom vanities, wet utility areas, sit-outs | High-heat zones next to the hob |
| Particle board | Factory flat-pack furniture | Humid regions and site carpentry |

Thickness: 18 (or 19) mm for carcass and shutters; 12 mm for some internal shelves and partitions; 6–8 mm for backs. Sheet size 8' × 4' (2440 × 1220). Some brands also sell 7' × 4', 8' × 3' and 6' × 3' — check availability before planning cut lists around them.

Commonly specified Indian plywood brands include Century, Greenply and Kitply; HDHMR is widely sold under Action TESA. Check grade certificates (IS mark) on delivery.

## Finishes

| Finish | Look | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Laminate (about 1 mm outside, 0.7–0.8 mm liner inside) | Solid colours, wood grains, textures | Most common; durable; edges banded |
| Acrylic | High-gloss, mirror-like | Premium kitchens; scratches show; use matching edge band |
| PU paint | Seamless matte or gloss in any colour | Premium; needs a good paint shop; touch-up possible |
| Veneer + PU/melamine polish | Natural wood | Use quarter-cut or crown-cut by design; matches teak doors |
| Lacquered glass / profile glass shutters | Accents, crockery uppers | Aluminium profile shutters for light weight |
| Membrane / thermofoil | Routed shutter designs on MDF/HDHMR | Avoid next to the hob (heat) |

Edge banding: 0.8 mm on internal edges, 2 mm on visible edges and shutters (match laminate colour).

Counters: granite is standard (black galaxy, absolute black, steel grey, tan brown); quartz for premium; avoid marble for Indian cooking (turmeric and oil stain it).

## Hardware

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Hinges | Soft-close concealed hinges; number per shutter grows with height and weight (check catalogue) |
| Drawer slides | Telescopic (ball-bearing) for budget; tandem / box systems (metal sides) for soft-close kitchens |
| Kitchen baskets | Thali, plate, cup-saucer, cutlery, plain baskets; SS 304 preferred in humid regions |
| Pull-outs | Oil/bottle (cabinet 150/200/300), tall pantry, under-sink, corner (magic corner, LeMans, carousel) |
| Lift-ups | Gas-strut or aventos-type lift systems for wall units |
| Wardrobe | Sliding tracks, trouser/tie/saree pull-outs, wardrobe lifts, LED profiles with sensors |
| Handles | Profile (G/J) handles, gola profiles, knobs, push-to-open |

Brands commonly specified in India: Hettich, Häfele, Ebco, Blum (and others). Match the grade to the budget; always check the catalogue for minimum cabinet width/depth before fixing module sizes.

## Colour and style quick guide
- Use 60-30-10 (wall / furniture & cabinets / accent). One undertone family per home.
- Kitchens: light easy-to-clean cabinets with dark granite, or wood with white quartz.
- Bedrooms: calm soft tones; Pooja: white, ivory, light yellow, warm gold with wood or marble.
- Tamil Nadu traditional: teak, brass, Athangudi tiles, maroon/mustard accents. Kerala: dark wood, laterite, terracotta, brass urulis and nilavilakku.
- Hot-humid climate: light colours, washable paint, anti-fungal paint in damp rooms; avoid wallpaper and MDF in damp Kerala rooms.
- Vastu colour associations are options when the client asks, never imposed.
